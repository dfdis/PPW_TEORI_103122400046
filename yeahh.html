01 / The interrogation

WHAT HAVE
YOU BEEN TOLD?

Fear spreads through stories. Facts ask us to look closer. Open a file and question what you think you know.

001 Sharks are man-eaters. +

THE FACT

Humans are not the natural prey of most shark species. Sharks may bite people in rare encounters, but the idea that they actively hunt humans is misleading.

002 Every shark is dangerous. +

THE FACT

Sharks vary enormously in size, diet, and behavior. Whale sharks, for example, feed mainly on tiny organisms by filtering food from the water.

003 Sharks have no purpose. +

THE FACT

Sharks play different roles in marine ecosystems. Depending on the species, they can influence food webs and contribute to the balance of ocean life.

004 Sharks are the real threat. +

THE FACT

Many shark populations face pressure from fishing, bycatch, and habitat changes. Understanding these threats helps us see why shark conservation matters.
